5. DESCRIPTION  (max 4000 characters)
----------------------------------------------------------------
DamnDeal is an online shopping app made for India. Browse thousands of products across fashion, electronics, home appliances, kitchen, beauty and daily essentials, compare prices, claim coupons, and get your order delivered to your door.

SHOP EVERYTHING IN ONE PLACE
- Fashion for men, women and kids
- Mobiles, laptops, audio and accessories
- Home appliances and kitchen products
- Beauty, personal care and daily needs
- New products added every week

DEALS AND COUPONS
- Deal of the Day and limited time offers
- Free coupons from brands near you, claim in one tap
- Discount codes that apply straight at checkout
- Price drop and clearance sections

EASY, SAFE CHECKOUT
- Cash on Delivery available
- Pay by UPI, debit card, credit card or net banking
- Payments handled by trusted payment partners
- Wallet balance and refunds in one place

TRACK EVERY ORDER
- Live order status from packing to delivery
- Full order history with invoices
- Simple returns and refund requests
- Help by phone, WhatsApp and email

BUILT FOR YOUR PHONE
- Native tab bar: Home, Categories, Offers, Cart and Account
- Offers tab with fresh banners and handpicked deals
- Pull to refresh on every main screen
- Quick Actions: long press the app icon to jump to Offers, My Orders or My Cart
- Works on slow networks and tells you clearly when you are offline

DELIVERY ACROSS INDIA
Enter your pincode, or tap to share your location, to check delivery availability and see the offers running near you. Location is requested only when you tap that button, never in the background.

We are the DamnDeal team and we run the damndeal.in store ourselves. If something is not right with an order, write to support@damndeal.in and a real person will reply.

Happy shopping.
