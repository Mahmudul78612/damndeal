================================================================================
                           DAMNDEAL — CONCEPT OVERVIEW
================================================================================

Platform Type : Hyperlocal Commerce & Delivery Platform
Tagline       : "Apne Aas-Paas Ki Dukaan, Ab Phone Pe"
Model         : B2B2C (Business to Business to Consumer)

================================================================================
1. IDEA IN SHORT
================================================================================

DamnDeal ek hyperlocal ecommerce platform hai jahan:
- Local dukandar (kirana, medical, electronics, bakery etc.) apni shop onboard karte hain
- Unke aas-paas ke log (20km radius) unki shop browse karte hain aur order karte hain
- Delivery boy jake delivery karta hai — ya toh platform ka ya dukandar ka apna
- Same-day delivery with real-time tracking

================================================================================
3. CORE FLOW (User Journey)
================================================================================

[1] User opens app
    |
[2] Location detect hoti hai (lat/lng)
    |
[3] 20km radius ke andar approved shops dikhti hain (geo search)
    |
[4] User shop select karta hai → products browse karta hai
    |
[5] Cart mein add karta hai → Coupon apply (optional)
    |
[6] Order place karta hai:
    - Delivery fee calculate hoti hai (distance based — Haversine formula)
    - Platform fee lagti hai (configurable %)
    - Free delivery agar order amount threshold cross kare
    - Wallet balance use kar sakta hai (partial/full)
    - GST auto-calculate per product
    |
[7] Payment: Razorpay (online) / COD / Wallet / Razorpay+Wallet combo
    |
[8] Partner ko notification aati hai (Socket.io real-time)
    |
[9] Partner 15 min ke andar accept/reject karta hai
    (Auto-reject agar 15 min mein koi action nahi)
    |
[10] Partner order ready mark karta hai
    |
[11] Delivery boy assign hota hai:
     - Platform delivery boy (admin/partner assign karta hai)
     - Self-ship (partner ka apna delivery boy)
    |
[12] Delivery boy pickup → shipped → delivered
     (Real-time location tracking via Socket.io)
    |
[13] Delivery OTP verify → Order delivered
    |
[14] User review de sakta hai (shop + delivery boy rating)
    |
[15] Return request bhi kar sakta hai (admin approve → wallet refund)

================================================================================
4. PARTNER FLOW
================================================================================

[1] Partner signup (phone OTP)
[2] KYC submit: name, org, GST, PAN, photo, shop address, location (lat/lng)
[3] Admin KYC approve karega
[4] Approved hone ke baad:
    - Products add karo (name, price, MRP, GST, stock, images)
    - Admin product approve karega → phir live hoga
    - Orders receive karo (app orders + POS billing apne counter se)
    - Apne delivery boys add karo (self-ship option)
    - Offers/deals create karo
    - Subscription plan lo (extra features ke liye)
    - Payouts track karo
    - Customers list dekho
    - Support tickets raise karo

6. KEY FEATURES
================================================================================

USERS:
  - Geo-based shop discovery (20km radius)
  - Cross-shop product search
  - Wishlist (products + shops save)
  - Wallet system (refund, cashback, referral bonus)
  - Coupon system (global, partner-specific, first-order, category-based)
  - Referral system (refer & earn — both get wallet credit)
  - Order tracking (real-time via Socket.io)
  - Return/refund requests
  - Invoice PDF download
  - Support tickets
  - Delivery time estimate
  - Multiple payment methods (Razorpay, COD, Wallet, Hybrid)

PARTNERS:
  - KYC verification
  - Product management (with GST, stock tracking, inventory logs)
  - POS billing (counter pe direct bill)
  - Order management (accept/reject/ready/assign delivery)
  - Self-ship — apne delivery boys se deliver karo
  - Own delivery fleet management
  - Offers/deals create (flash deal, combo, BOGO, flat discount)
  - Subscription plans
  - Customer database
  - Payout tracking
  - Invoice generation
  - Support tickets

ADMIN:
  - Full dashboard (orders, revenue, users, partners stats)
  - KYC approve/reject
  - Product approve/reject
  - Coupon CRUD (global + scoped)
  - Offer management
  - Return approve/reject (wallet refund + stock restore)
  - Reports (orders, revenue, users, payments — CSV export)
  - Wallet management (view, credit to any user)
  - Subscription plan management
  - Staff management (permission-based)
  - Delivery boy management (verify, toggle)
  - Banner management
  - Home page sections arrangement
  - App settings (delivery fee, platform fee, free delivery threshold)
  - Notification management
  - Support ticket management (assign, reply, resolve, close)
  - Payout management

DELIVERY:
  - Profile & documents
  - Assignment management
  - Earnings tracking
  - Live location broadcast (Socket.io)

SYSTEM:
  - Cron Jobs:
    * Auto-cancel unpaid orders (30 min)
    * Auto-reject unaccepted orders (15 min)
    * Expire subscriptions (daily)
    * Deactivate expired coupons (daily)
    * Deactivate expired offers (daily)
  - Image optimization (Sharp — auto WebP conversion)
  - Rate limiting (OTP: 10/15min, API: 200/15min)
  - SMS/Email/Push notification templates (plug-and-play)

================================================================================
7. TECH STACK
================================================================================

Backend       : Node.js 20 + Express 4.21
Database      : MongoDB 7 (Mongoose 8.7)
Cache/OTP     : Redis 7
Auth          : JWT (access 15min + refresh 30d) + Phone OTP
Payment       : Razorpay SDK
Real-time     : Socket.io (JWT authenticated)
File Upload   : Multer + Sharp (image optimization → WebP)
PDF           : PDFKit (invoices)
Cron          : node-cron
Containerized : Docker Compose (API + MongoDB + Redis)
Security      : Helmet, CORS, Rate Limiting, bcryptjs
