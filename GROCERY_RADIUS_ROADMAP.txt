DECISIONS (aapse confirm ho chuke)
  Store model   : apne dark stores + partner vendors, dono
  Serviceability: circle radius in km from the store pin
  Region        : damndeal.in (IN) aur damndeal.com (US) dono
  Quality bar   : "best wala system as its blinkit"


----------------------------------------------------------------
PEHLE SE KYA HAI (verify karke likha, guess nahi)
----------------------------------------------------------------
[HAI] DDGo hi grocery platform hai. Koi alag module nahi —
      `platform: 'ddgo' | 'damndeal'` field in par lagta hai:
      Product, Category, Banner, HomeSection, DesktopHomeSection.
      Grocery categories (Vegetables & Fruits, Atta/Dail/Rice,
      Chips & Namkeen, Tea/Coffee, Bath & Body …) already
      regions:["IN"] pe hain.

[HAI] Partner shop ka location — PartnerKyc.location (GeoJSON
      Point) + 2dsphere index. Shop ka pin already stored hai.

[HAI] Nearby shops — shop.controller.js geo query se distance
      ke saath shops laata hai.

[HAI] Radius check — fee.service.js me:
        distanceKm > maxRadius  →  "Delivery not available
        beyond X km"

[HAI] DDGo fee settings — admin → Quick Commerce Settings:
        ddgo_min_order_amount, ddgo_delivery_fee,
        ddgo_delivery_fee_per_km, ddgo_free_delivery_above,
        ddgo_platform_fee, ddgo_max_delivery_radius


----------------------------------------------------------------
KYA NAHI HAI (yahi banana hai)
----------------------------------------------------------------
[BLOCKER] Radius EK GLOBAL NUMBER hai, per-store nahi.
      `ddgo_max_delivery_radius` poore platform pe ek hi value.
      Blinkit me har dark store apna radius rakhta hai — 5 km
      dense city me, 12 km outskirts pe. Abhi wo possible nahi.

[BLOCKER] Apne dark stores ka koi concept hi nahi. Har store
      ek partner se juda hai (PartnerKyc). DamnDeal khud ka
      store nahi bana sakta.

[BLOCKER] Serviceability check SIRF CHECKOUT PE hota hai.
      Customer poora catalogue browse karta hai, cart bharta
      hai, aur checkout pe pata chalta hai "not available".
      Blinkit pehle location poochta hai, phir wahi dikhata
      hai jo mil sakta hai.

[BLOCKER] US pe radius bypass hai. fee.service me:
        maxRadius = isUS ? Number.MAX_SAFE_INTEGER : …
      US CJ dropshipping ke liye bana tha. DDGo US pe chalane
      se pehle ye hatana padega, warna koi radius lagega hi
      nahi.

[GAP] PartnerKyc me `regions` field nahi — partner shop IN ka
      hai ya US ka, ye batane ka tarika nahi.

[GAP] Store timings nahi. Band store bhi serviceable dikhega.

[GAP] Per-store stock nahi. Product ka stock product pe hai,
      store pe nahi — do stores ka alag stock possible nahi.

PHASE 1 — Store + serviceability core                 [HO GAYA]
----------------------------------------------------------------
 1.1  DarkStore model — apne stores ke liye
        name, code, location (Point + 2dsphere), radiusKm,
        address/city/state/pincode, timings, isActive,
        regions, priority, contact
 1.2  PartnerKyc me deliveryRadiusKm + regions add
 1.3  serviceability.service.js — ek hi resolver jo dark
        stores aur partner shops dono dekhta hai, har ek ka
        APNA radius lagata hai, nearest serving store return
        karta hai (distance + ETA ke saath)
 1.4  GET /user/serviceability?lat&lng  (public)
 1.5  Admin: dark store CRUD API + "Stores" page
        (pin + radius + timings, live coverage preview)
 1.6  Partner: apna radius set karne ka API
 1.7  fee.service — global radius ki jagah resolved store ka
        radius; US bypass hatao jab platform=ddgo ho
