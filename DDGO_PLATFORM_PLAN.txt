ARCHITECTURE — ek zaroori faisla
----------------------------------------------------------------
ALAG PANEL = HAAN.  ALAG DATABASE = NAHI.

  DDGo admin panel aur store portal alag UI honge (aapne bola,
  aur sahi bhi hai — quick commerce ka kaam alag hai).

  LEKIN dono usi backend/models pe chalenge jo already live aur
  tested hai: Product, Order, PartnerKyc, DeliveryBoy, Payout +
  naye DarkStore, StoreInventory.

  Kyun: alag database banane se ek hi cheez do jagah rehti hai —
  ek order do jagah, ek payout do jagah. Wo hamesha aage jaake
  aapas me mismatch karta hai. Panel alag, data ek.

  Store ke do type, dono ek hi serviceability resolver se:
    - DarkStore   = hamara apna store
    - PartnerKyc  = onboard kiya hua vendor (geo + radius + region)
